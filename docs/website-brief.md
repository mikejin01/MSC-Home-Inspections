## 4. Brand & Design Direction

### 4.1 Color Palette

Sampled from the flyer. Hex values are close approximations — adjust against the source file if exact matching is needed.

| Role | Color | Hex (approx.) | Usage on flyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary — Deep Navy** | ⬛ | `#0E2135` | Dark banner bands, hero overlay panel, footer bar, logo roof outline |
| **Primary — Near-Black Navy** | ⬛ | `#0B1622` | "MSC" wordmark, heaviest text |
| **Accent — Brand Blue** | 🟦 | `#1C6FC9` | Icon circles, "SO YOU CAN," section headings, "That's the MSC Difference," CTA heading |
| **Accent — Bright Blue (hover/highlight)** | 🟦 | `#2E86E0` | Lighter blue highlights within gradients |
| **Neutral — White** | ⬜ | `#FFFFFF` | Card backgrounds, headline text on dark |
| **Neutral — Off-White / Card Gray** | ⬜ | `#F3F5F8` | Page background, service card fills |
| **Neutral — Body Text Gray** | ⬛ | `#2B3440` | Service card body copy |
| **Divider / Rule** | ⬜ | `#C9D2DC` | Thin rules under service headings |

**Palette logic:** a two-tone blue system — deep navy for authority/structure, bright blue for emphasis and interaction — on a white/off-white base. There is **no third accent color** (no orange, green, or red). Keep it that way; the restraint is what makes it read as professional rather than promotional.

**Suggested web application:**
- Navy `#0E2135` → header/nav, footer, section bands, primary text
- Brand blue `#1C6FC9` → buttons, links, icons, active states, key phrase highlights within headlines
- White / `#F3F5F8` → alternating section backgrounds
- Use a subtle navy gradient (`#0E2135` → `#16304C`) for hero overlays, matching the flyer's hero panel

### 4.2 Typography

The flyer uses a **three-tier type system**. Exact fonts aren't identifiable from the image, so each is paired with a close, freely-licensed web equivalent.

| Tier | Style on flyer | Web font recommendation | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | Heavy **condensed** sans, all caps, tight tracking — "WE LOOK DEEPER," "SCHEDULE YOUR INSPECTION TODAY!," service card titles | **Oswald** (700) — alternates: Archivo Narrow Bold, Big Shoulders Display, Barlow Condensed Bold | H1–H3, service card titles, CTA headings, banner text |
| **Logo / Brand Wordmark** | Heavy geometric sans, wide letterspacing on "HOME INSPECTIONS" | **Montserrat** (700–800) — alternate: Poppins Bold | Logo lockup only (ideally use the actual logo asset, not live text) |
| **Body** | Clean humanist sans, regular weight, comfortable line height | **Open Sans** (400/600) — alternates: Source Sans 3, Inter | Paragraphs, service descriptions, form labels, footer |
| **Script Accent** | Casual connected brush script — "Protecting What Matters Most." | **Yellowtail** — alternates: Kaushan Script, Norican | Sparingly: one tagline in the hero and possibly one section intro. Never for body copy or buttons. |

**Type rules observed on the flyer:**
- Headlines are **all caps**, condensed, and often **two-tone** — part of the line in white/navy, the emphasized part in brand blue ("WE LOOK DEEPER / **SO YOU CAN** / BUY WITH CONFIDENCE"). Carry this two-tone headline treatment onto the site; it's the most distinctive typographic move in the brand.
- Body copy is centered inside service cards, left-aligned in the hero.
- A thin horizontal rule sits between each service card title and its body text.

### 4.3 Logo

- **Lockup:** a stylized navy roof/house outline (two overlapping rooflines with a small four-pane window) sitting above the "MSC" wordmark, with "HOME INSPECTIONS" beneath it and "LLC" set inside a horizontal rule at the bottom.
- **Colors:** navy roof mark on white; near-black wordmark.
- **Variants needed for the site:** full-color on white (primary), all-white reversed version for the navy header/footer. The polo shirt in the photo shows a compact reversed version already exists.
- **[Action item]** Request the original vector logo (`.svg`, `.ai`, or `.eps`) — the flyer JPEG isn't a usable source for a crisp header logo or favicon.

### 4.4 Imagery Style

The flyer's photography splits into three clear categories — mirror this on the site:

1. **Hero / aspirational:** a well-kept suburban home at dusk with warm interior lights glowing. Conveys the outcome the customer wants. Use as the hero background behind a navy overlay panel.
2. **Evidence / problem imagery:** tight, unglamorous documentary shots of actual defects — mold on a ceiling corner, a water-damaged collapsed ceiling, an attic interior, a crawl space with vapor barrier, a mold culture in a petri dish. These make the specialty credible. Use as service card thumbnails.
3. **Human / trust:** a cutout portrait of the inspector in a branded black MSC polo, no background. Builds the owner-operated, know-who-shows-up trust factor. Use on an "About / Meet Your Inspector" section.

**Icon style:** simple white line-art glyphs inside solid blue filled circles. Keep icons single-color, stroked, and circular-badged for consistency.

### 4.5 Layout & Visual Language

Patterns from the flyer worth carrying into the site:

- **Alternating light/dark bands.** White or off-white content sections separated by full-width deep-navy bands. Gives rhythm and lets the CTA bands stand out.
- **Angled/notched panel edge.** The hero text panel has a distinctive angled notch where it meets the inspector photo. A subtle diagonal or clipped edge on the hero is an easy, faithful signature detail.
- **Card grid for services.** Five equal cards: image on top, blue circular icon overlapping the image's lower edge, all-caps blue title, thin rule, then centered body copy. Translates directly to a responsive 5-up → 2-up → 1-up grid.
- **Split trust bar.** A navy band divided by a vertical rule: a shield-check icon with "Detailed. Professional. Reliable." on one side, a circle-check icon with "Serving the New Jersey Area" on the other.
- **Contact footer.** Circular icon badges (phone, envelope) beside small all-caps labels with large contact values underneath, above a thin navy footer strip with the full business name letterspaced.
- **Overall feel:** clean, high-contrast, confident, generous whitespace. Corporate-professional rather than folksy. Nothing rounded, playful, or pastel.
